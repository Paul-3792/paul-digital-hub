How I Started Freelancing in Tech from Abuja

My freelancing journey started with curiosity and consistency. I spent time learning web development, practicing daily, and improving my skills little by little.

Learning the Skills

I started with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Watching tutorials helped, but building projects helped even more.

Getting My First Client

My first clients came from social media and referrals. I shared my work consistently and people started noticing my skills.

Challenges

Freelancing is not always easy. Sometimes there are no clients, delayed payments, or difficult projects. But consistency and improvement always pay off.

Lessons I Learned

Build your skills, stay patient, and market yourself online. Opportunities come when people can clearly see the value you provide.
