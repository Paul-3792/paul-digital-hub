5 Signs Your Phone Needs a Repair Before It Breaks

Most phones show warning signs before they stop working completely. Fixing problems early can save you money and prevent bigger damage.

1. Fast Battery Drain

If your battery drops quickly even after charging fully, your battery may need replacement.

2. Overheating

Phones that heat up constantly may have battery or software issues that need attention.

3. Cracked Screen

Small cracks can become worse over time and may affect touch performance.

4. Slow Performance

Lagging apps and freezing screens may be caused by storage problems or outdated software.

5. Charging Problems

If your charger works only at certain angles, your charging port may need repair.
